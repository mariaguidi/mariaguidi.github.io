Ciao! I'm Maria Guidi, a postdoctoral researcher in Federico Giove's lab (MARBILab, Enrico Fermi Research Center and Fondazione Santa Lucia IRCCS, Rome), investigating brain physiology with fMRI. I have a background in physics and did my PhD under the supervision of Harald E. Möller at the NMR Unit of the Max Planck Institute for Human Cognitive and Brain Sciences in Leipzig.

Separating the neuronal from the vascular component of the BOLD response is probably my main interest, and the techniques I like are:

  • VASO
  • ASL
  • calibrated BOLD
  • gas challenges for cerebrovascular reactivity (CVR) mapping

I used them at 7 T during my PhD and I am now applying them at 3 T, in healthy aging, mild cognitive impairment and Alzheimer's disease.
